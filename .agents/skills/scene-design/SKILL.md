# Scene Design

## Output

Rows in `.studio/internal/story/scene-cards.csv` and corresponding stable sections in `03-script/script.md`.

## Method

Record entry state, purpose, story value, production requirements, and exit state for every scene. Use objective, obstacle, tactics, turn, and outcome where they clarify the scene engine; treat them as diagnostics rather than mandatory laws.

Track literal words, speaker objective, fear, tactic, concealed information, relationship status, power shift, and subtext for important dialogue. Begin near meaningful friction and leave after decisive change unless atmosphere, ritual, anticipation, contrast, aftermath, setup, or thematic value justifies more duration.

## Audit question

If the scene is removed, what later event, character understanding, emotional progression, world knowledge, setup, payoff, visual rhythm, or production transition becomes weaker or impossible?
