# Sound Design

## Output
`08-edit/sound-plan.md` plus asset-register entries.

## Method
Give locations and threats identifiable sonic rules. Map ambience and perspective across beats. Use music for structural/emotional function, not continuous wallpaper. Treat silence as an authored cue. Track source/licence and replacement status for every external sound/music asset.

## QC
Check intelligibility, clipping, noise, transitions, loudness consistency, mono/small-speaker translation, and whether sound reveals information too early.
