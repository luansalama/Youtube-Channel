# Research Ledger

## Outputs
`02-research/source-ledger.csv`, `claims.md`, and when relevant `inspiration-analysis.md`.

## Method
Turn the concept into decision-changing questions. Prefer primary/official sources, then reputable specialist sources. For each row record source ID, exact claim, URL, publisher, dates, type, reliability, status, and notes. Cross-check high-impact claims. Separate canon from real-world assertion.

## Never
Cite an AI answer, invent metadata, hide contradictions, or copy long copyrighted passages. Mark `unresolved` when evidence is inadequate.
