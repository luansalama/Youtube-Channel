# Continuity Control

## Source of truth

`.studio/internal/story/story-model.json`, `continuity-bible.md`, `information-map.csv`, `setup-payoff-ledger.csv`, and stable IDs in causal, beat, scene, shot, capture, and edit files.

## Method

Record rules before exceptions. Maintain chronological timeline separately from audience presentation. Track location graph, travel constraints, inventory/resources, injuries, abilities, relationship states, and who knows each fact at every important point.

For every exceptional Minecraft/world rule, record who knows it, how the audience sees proof, what it permits, what it prevents, and what happens when it is broken.

A proposed canon change must record reason, affected upstream decision, downstream script/shot/set/capture/edit/caption/packaging impact, uncertainty, and human decision. Reopen before editing locked evidence.

## Failure condition

Do not patch a contradiction by adding unexplained lore. Return to the earliest invalid assumption, causal link, world rule, or knowledge transfer.
