# Audience Information Design

## Output

`.studio/internal/story/information-map.csv`, relevant setup/payoff rows, and information sections in scene cards and script.

## Method

For every important question or fact record what the audience knows, expects, fears, and hopes before delivery; what relevant characters know; how the information is shown; what changes after delivery; and which earlier setups make the reveal fair.

A twist should be difficult to predict but clearer in retrospect. Do not create surprise by making necessary evidence impossible to infer. Distinguish deliberate uncertainty from accidental confusion.

## Effects

Use suspense for uncertain consequential outcomes, curiosity for missing causes or explanations, dramatic irony when the audience knows more than a character, and mystery when the audience reconstructs concealed events, motives, identities, or rules.
