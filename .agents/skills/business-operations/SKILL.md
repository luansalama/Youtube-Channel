# Business Operations

## Sources
`data/business/ledger.csv`, video budgets/postmortems, and monthly-review template.

## Method
Record revenue/expense in original currency, category, video, counterparty, and note. Track creator-hours separately in production documents. Calculate cash profit by currency; do not silently convert. Evaluate catalogue/reuse value as well as immediate return.

## Decision
Recommend one allocation change per review, with evidence, risk, owner, and review date.
