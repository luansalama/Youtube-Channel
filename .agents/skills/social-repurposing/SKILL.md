# Social Repurposing

## Output
`.studio/internal/release/social-pack.md` and referenced derivative assets.

## Method
Choose moments that retain context and point back to the full work. Adapt hook and length per platform without inventing a stronger promise. Include community post, short teaser, text posts, caption, and behind-the-scenes angle. Record rights and source master.

## Boundary
Draft only. Do not post, message, scrape audiences, or automate engagement without a separately approved connector and per-post review.
