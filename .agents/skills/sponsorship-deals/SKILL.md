# Sponsorship Deals

## Outputs
Deal ledger entry and `templates/business/deal-brief.md` copy.

## Review
Assess audience fit, creator credibility, deliverables, placement, revision cap, substantiation of claims, usage/paid-media rights, term, territory, exclusivity, fee, currency, tax, payment schedule, cancellation, and opportunity cost.

## Boundary
Use verified metrics only. Draft outreach or counterterms but never send, accept, sign, invoice, or commit without Luan's explicit instruction and professional review where appropriate.
