# Production script — The Portal That Nobody Made

> Draft ID: `draft-02`. This revision follows the current unlocked echo-reality Story proposal and the latest script note. It records no creative lock or production validation.

## Status and use

- Editorial target: approximately 20 minutes. Scene timings are planning guides, not measured results or structural quotas.
- `R0` to `R4` are crew labels and are never spoken.
- One creator-voiced, third-person narrator follows the protagonist’s understanding. The on-screen protagonist does not speak.
- Crying-obsidian portal behaviour is invented story canon. Seamless portal views are planned with the Immersive Portals mod; compatibility and capture behaviour have not been verified for the selected Minecraft version.
- Minecraft fire, wolf, creeper and charged-anchor staging must be tested before capture.

## Continuity keys

- **match cut** is the same camera position, lens, path and direction of travel in Scenes 1 and 2. R4 shows the later smoke and developed landscape; R0 shows the fresh clearing.
- Every ravine contains the same lightning-shaped granite seam. It is the first orientation anchor in each echo.
- The protagonist’s survey journal begins mostly blank, with a stitched repair on one corner and a three-line ravine symbol. The Weeping Atlas is a weathered counterpart with the same repair, symbol and handwriting.
- For crew continuity, the wolf is called Moss throughout. No in-world name appears above him until the Scene 2 name-tag beat.
- Moss’s rug is moss-green wool. Its final added square sits nearest the open hearth and provides the proposed fire path.
- Echo portals use crying obsidian, directional tear-like particles and a low layered hum. Their centres show seamless views of the destination rather than opaque purple fields.
- The conventional Nether portal used for supply journeys has ordinary obsidian and familiar audio.
- Moss’s R4 fate remains unseen. Show no body, collar drop or death message.
- The final falling-creeper route needs a believable dark ledge above the activation position. The portal response to its blast reaching the charged frame is invented canon, not a claimed vanilla mechanic.
