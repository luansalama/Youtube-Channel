# Pronunciation and language guide

- Delivery accent: creator's natural English; do not imitate an accent.
- Spelling convention: British English.
- These cues support consistency only; the creator’s natural, intelligible delivery takes priority.

## Names and terms

- **Moss** — /mɒs/; short vowel, like the plant.
- **ravine** — /rə-VEE-n/; stress the second syllable.
- **obsidian** — /əb-SID-ee-ən/.
- **crying obsidian** — /KRY-ing əb-SID-ee-ən/; keep the two words separate.
- **Nether** — /NETH-ə/ in a non-rhotic delivery; use the creator’s natural final sound.
- **respawn** — /ree-SPAWN/.
- **glowstone** — /GLOH-stohn/.
- **echo** — /EK-oh/; plural **echoes**, /EK-ohz/.
- **Atlas** — /AT-ləs/. The full production name **Weeping Atlas** is /WEE-ping AT-ləs/.
- **threshold** — /THRESH-hohld/.
- **mouths** — /maʊðz/ in the phrase **one frame, many mouths**; use the voiced final sound.

## Delivery and emphasis

- **Same numbers. Wrong history.** — allow a short pause between the sentences; do not overplay **wrong**.
- **Next mouth. Never previous.** — clear informational delivery, with light stress on **next** and **never**.
- **First threshold** — stress **first** only when contrasting it with the next mouth.
- **The hand that discharges the anchor becomes the key.** — stress **hand** and **key**; avoid rushing **discharges the anchor**.
- **I chose the chance I could touch.** — keep restrained; the image supplies the emotion.
- The repeated line **Moss sat down before I did. I took it as a vote. We could stay.** must use the identical recording at the beginning and ending.

## Crew-only labels

- `R0` to `R4` are pronounced **ar-zero** to **ar-four** in production discussion.
- They do not appear in narration or on-screen story text.

## Translation notes

No translated dialogue is currently planned. Preserve the distinction between a conventional Nether portal, an unanchored echo mouth and the keyed final frame in any subtitles or localisation.
