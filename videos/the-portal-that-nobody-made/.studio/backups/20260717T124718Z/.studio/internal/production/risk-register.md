## Scope tripwires

- Do not build all five echoes before the R0-to-R1 portal weather wolf and capture test produces evidence or a documented fallback.
- Do not commission or build a custom mod before a recorded test identifies a precise missing capability.
- Do not use unrelated seeds for echo variation.
- Do not add shaders or unrelated tools before the minimum stack is stable and backed up.
- Do not capture direct anchor discharge as story action.
- Do not add a surface portal hum in R0.
- Do not burn break or explode the only copy of a set state.
- Do not use third-party media or commission paid work without verified rights and human approval. Record costs in BRL.
- Do not claim that echo frames keyed routes or creeper-triggered activation are vanilla behaviour.
- Stop and restore a backup after unexplained world entity or portal corruption.

## Rights and recovery controls

1. Obtain mods only from the official project or release pages recorded in the source ledger.
2. Record each exact filename version source licence and hash before use.
3. Keep original assets configuration and commands separate from third-party binaries.
4. Preserve a clean profile an untouched master world and verified echo milestones.
5. Keep crash reports logs screenshots and concise reproduction notes for every blocker.
6. Test version changes on copies rather than the only production state.
