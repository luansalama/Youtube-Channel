## Totals and interpretation

- Must packages: approximately 212 hours.
- Should packages: approximately 26 hours.
- Could packages: approximately 6 hours.
- Full planning envelope: approximately 244 solo hours.
- Authorised cash: BRL 0.

The largest saving is structural: the ridge, clearing, ravine, portal column and distant landscape are found or imported once, corrected in R0 and duplicated. R4 reuses R0 geography and adds Home rather than becoming another world build.

## Cut line

1. Remove shaders and path-traced hero shots.
2. Reduce generated-sketch/storyboard conversion; retain text and greybox guidance.
3. Reduce background dressing outside hero wedges.
4. Keep R2/R3 compact and imply scale through weather, darkness, sound and silhouettes.
5. Reduce redundant montage coverage while preserving causal Moss and Home beats.
6. Replace the literal Scene 13 rise with shorter modular dissolves.

Do not cut the shared geography continuity, `MATCH PATH`, `MATCH FRAME A`, marker absences, Atlas evidence, Home/fire cause, unresolved Moss search, creeper route, fatal interruption or delayed R0 reveal.
