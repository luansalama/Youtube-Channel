# Location plan

> Planned scouting, adaptation and modular production design only. No final seed, coordinate, imported region, custom dimension or completed build is evidenced.

## Core decision: find R0, do not build five worlds

Search existing seeds and generated locations for the closest usable ridge–clearing–ravine relationship. The chosen location becomes R0 after corrections. It does not need to contain every scripted feature naturally.

The approved R0 must support, through existing terrain or bounded imports:

- the `MATCH PATH` ridge/homestead approach;
- the R0 clearing, overlook, fishing bank and `MATCH FRAME A`;
- a descent route and recognisable forked granite seam;
- the exposed-iron turn and portal chamber;
- a corresponding surface column suitable for R1/R2/R4 variations;
- camera corridors for portal parallax, match inserts and Replay paths.

Record candidates in `seed-scouting-log.csv`. Prefer the lowest total edit distance, clean camera access and strong silhouettes over a seed that is technically impressive but difficult to control.

## Import policy

If the strongest candidate lacks a ravine, cave opening, forest mass, mountain profile or structure, import the missing feature from another seed with MCA Selector or WorldEdit. Preserve source/provenance, create a pre-import backup and treat the imported section as raw material. Blend terrain, biome, lighting, water, vegetation and chunk boundaries before approving R0.

A seed number may be used as a generation reference. A downloaded authored map or schematic must have source and usage-rights evidence before publication use.

## R0 duplication point

Complete only the expensive shared geography before duplication:

- terrain silhouette and horizon blockers;
- ravine/descent and seam;
- portal chamber and surface relationship;
- hero forest/water masses;
- shared camera corridors and match marks;
- all chunks that may appear in camera.

Then preserve `TPNM_R0_SOURCE` and create R0–R4 working saves. Small R0-only props can be added later. Common changes after duplication should use schematics or controlled region replacement.

## Reality derivation

### R0

The approved scouted/adapted location. Protect fresh arrival, survival, wild wolf, tamed unnamed Moss, named Moss and `MATCH FRAME A` milestones.

### R1

Direct R0 duplicate. Add rain, waterfall/opening, dense wet vegetation, changed ground treatment, absent home and the first marker. Do not replace the shared terrain silhouette unnecessarily.

### R2

Direct R0 duplicate. Add ash/scorch, reduced vegetation, an exposed shelf and marker states. Keep the production wedge compact.

### R3

Direct R0 duplicate. Add the archive recess, desk/storage, gravel states and failing mouth. Suggest scale through darkness instead of building a complex.

### R4

Direct R0 duplicate and closest visual relative: `R4 = R0 + Home + minor seasonal/state differences`. Add the house, domestic growth, optional season distinction, fire/search states and final chamber. Do not rebuild the landscape.

## Pregeneration and filming master

Before duplication, use Chunky to pregenerate every chunk visible through portals, landscape shots, routes and Replay cameras, plus a safety buffer. Do not depend on new custom-dimension generation for a camera-visible boundary.

Create `TPNM_FILMING_MASTER` from R0. Register R1–R4 dimensions, visit each once, close Minecraft and transfer the approved terrain at zero offset. Use `region` by default. Transfer `entities` or `poi` only for explicit tested needs. Validate every dimension after two reloads, then stage Moss/actors and create portals last.

## Modular build strategy

### Terrain modules

- Imported or existing ravine wall and forked seam: shared source geometry.
- Replaceable roof opening/waterfall and wet vegetation: R1.
- Replaceable scorched shelf, tree silhouettes and ash emitters: R2.
- Compact survey recess and gravel stair: R3.
- Incomplete, complete, active and aftermath portal-chamber states: bounded modules.
- Surface dressing rings: near-camera hero ring, midground identity ring and silhouette-only background ring.

### Homestead modules

- House positioned around the inherited R0 `MATCH PATH` geography.
- Swappable wall/roof milestones.
- Shared-room floor cassette containing bare, first-rug, final-rug, first-flame and burned-fan states.
- Stone hearth on one invariant mark.
- Removable shortest wall for Scene 10.
- Independent furniture/storage dressing so forbidden items can be inspected.

### Lighting and access

Keep a camera corridor behind every hero angle and hide incomplete surfaces outside the maximum tested FOV. Fix common time through the take controller; use dimension-specific weather and optional season only after testing. Build ceilings only where visible or mechanically required.

## Build order

1. Run the disposable R0/R1 custom-dimension transfer proof.
2. Scout and shortlist locations; approve the lowest-edit R0 candidate.
3. Import or sculpt only the missing shared geography.
4. Mark camera corridors and pregenerate the complete production footprint.
5. Preserve `TPNM_R0_SOURCE`; duplicate R0–R4 working saves.
6. Build/capture R0 milestones.
7. Dress R1 and validate weather; dress R2/R3 minimally after portal method selection.
8. Derive R4 directly from R0; preserve fresh R4 before the house.
9. Advance R4 through home, rug, pre-fire, fire and burned milestones.
10. Assemble the filming master and validate transferred dimensions before portals/entities.
11. Build final chamber, baffle and creeper ledge in sacrificial copies.
12. Perform Script, continuity and portal-routing review before set lock.

## Blender versus in-game split

Prefer Minecraft for terrain, recognisable geography, portals, domestic states and performances. Use Blender/compositing for portal cards/depth occlusion, smoke/particles, exact camera matching and Scene 13 modules when they are faster or safer than expanding the world.

## Reuse after this video

High-reuse elements are the scouting scorecard, R0-source naming discipline, terrain-import workflow, custom-dimension transfer checklist, portal chamber, camera-mark system, shared-room cassette and take-controller conventions. Reuse must not overwrite protected milestones or carry unclear third-party map rights.
