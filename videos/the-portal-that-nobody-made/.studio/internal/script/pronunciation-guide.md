# Pronunciation and language guide

- Delivery accent: creator's natural English; do not imitate an accent.
- Spelling convention: British English.
- These cues support consistency only; the creator's natural, intelligible delivery takes priority.

## Names and terms

- **Moss** — /mɒs/; short vowel, like the plant.
- **ravine** — /rə-VEE-n/; stress the second syllable.
- **obsidian** — /əb-SID-ee-ən/.
- **crying obsidian** — /KRY-ing əb-SID-ee-ən/; keep the two words separate.
- **Nether** — /NETH-ə/ in a non-rhotic delivery; use the creator's natural final sound.
- **respawn** — /ree-SPAWN/.
- **glowstone** — /GLOH-stohn/.
- **echo** — /EK-oh/; plural **echoes**, /EK-ohz/.
- **Atlas** — /AT-ləs/. The production name **Weeping Atlas** is /WEE-ping AT-ləs/.
- **threshold** — /THRESH-hohld/.
- **mouths** — /maʊðz/ in **one frame, many mouths**; use the voiced final sound.
- **baffle** — /BAF-əl/.
- **ignition** — /ig-NISH-ən/.

## Delivery and emphasis

- **He liked the place. So he put his pack down.** — keep plain and decisive; do not romanticise the pause.
- **Once could be loss. Twice was a rule.** — allow the second sentence to land without raising volume.
- **Same numbers. Wrong history.** — use a short pause; do not overplay **wrong**.
- **Next mouth. Never previous.** — clear informational delivery, with light stress on **next** and **never**.
- **The repair was his. So was the hand. The years between them were not.** — keep the three clauses measured rather than ominous.
- **The torn page ended where safety should have begun.** — stress **safety** lightly; avoid announcing the later death.
- **Below the hill was only another day's work. They went together.** — use the identical recording in Scenes 2 and 13. The first delivery should remain ordinary enough to acquire its second meaning through the image.
- There is no narration after the protagonist's death.

## Crew-only labels

- `R0` to `R4` are pronounced **ar-zero** to **ar-four** in production discussion.
- They do not appear in narration or on-screen story text.

## Translation notes

No translated dialogue is currently planned. Preserve the distinction between a conventional Nether portal, an unkeyed echo mouth and the builder-keyed final frame in any subtitles or localisation.

The Scene 12 bark is a subjective repetition of the Scene 9 sound, not dialogue, a supernatural message or proof of Moss's location.
