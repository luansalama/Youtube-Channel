# Inspiration analysis

## What the reference achieves

This research pass used philosophical and physics sources as **concept references**, not story references.

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy distinguishes changing a single past from participating in it. In a one-past model, a traveller cannot make an established event different, but can be one of the causes that made the event occur. This preserves consequential action without requiring branching timelines.

Friedman, Morris, Novikov and colleagues describe a proposed self-consistency principle for models containing closed timelike curves: local events must fit a globally consistent history. Their paper is theoretical physics research, not evidence that a Minecraft portal or practical backwards time travel can exist.

## Transferable principles

- Use one history seen from more than one personal perspective.
- Let the protagonist’s actions cause details that previously looked like interference.
- Preserve agency by distinguishing changing an event from participating in it.
- Make consistency emerge through ordinary causes, incomplete knowledge and decisions rather than an invisible temporal police force.
- Track the protagonist’s personal order separately from world chronology and audience presentation.
- Treat an apparent repeat as a revisit to one event, not an automatic reset.
- Allow the final image to remain physically unchanged while its meaning changes through new information.

## Elements not to copy

- No plot, character, setting, dialogue, image or reveal sequence was taken from an existing time-travel story.
- Do not reproduce scientific examples, equations or technical terminology in the narration unless later research establishes a genuine need.
- Do not claim that the self-consistency principle proves backwards time travel is possible.
- Do not use academic authority as a substitute for explaining the fictional portal rules through action.
- Do not rely on a grandfather-paradox scenario; the Minecraft story needs its own personal and visual stakes.

## Our production translation

The same ravine and homestead will appear in several controlled states. Persistent block arrangements, damage and the protagonist’s habits will carry the mystery visually. The protagonist first assumes he is encountering retries or an unknown traveller, then learns that each sighting belongs to one world history and one personal life viewed out of order.

The portal’s time-travel function remains invented canon. Minecraft mechanics will instead ground the consequences: the portal must be physically constructed from traceable materials, location changes must be reproducible, and the homestead’s destruction must follow a version-verified in-game causal chain. The narrator explains interpretation and emotion but does not deliver the solution before the images earn it.
