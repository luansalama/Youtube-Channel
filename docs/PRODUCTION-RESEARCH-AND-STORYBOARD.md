# Production research and storyboard workflow

## What belongs where

- `04-production/production-plan.md`: authoritative practical plan and phase-completion record.
- `.studio/internal/production/storyboard-plan.md`: readable visual grammar and scene-by-scene previsualisation.
- `technical-research.md`: version/mod/API decisions, evidence boundaries and deterministic take-control architecture.
- `toolchain-matrix.csv`: exact candidates, dependencies, licences, decisions and fallbacks.
- `scene-implementation-matrix.csv`: one implementation decision for every scripted scene.
- `test-matrix.csv`: proof-of-concept procedures and pass/fail evidence.
- `storyboard-panels.csv`: literal panel-level camera and blocking ledger.
- Existing shot, camera, location, asset, budget, risk and build records remain operational evidence.
- `capture-log.csv` remains real footage evidence and is never written by the assistant.

## Dashboard run

1. Open the active video's Production workspace.
2. Select **Develop phase**.
3. Select **Allow web search**.
4. Paste the prompt below and run Studio Assistant.
5. Review all changed files before accepting. A long proposal is expected.
6. Do not advance to Edit. First run the proof-of-concept tests and record genuine results.

## Prompt

Perform a research-first deepening pass for the Production phase of **The Portal That Nobody Made**. Treat the authoritative `draft-03` Script as absolute authority and preserve the existing thirteen-scene chronology, causality, continuity and unresolved fate of Moss.

Research current official or primary sources for every technical decision that materially affects the plan. Compare the strongest exact-version intersections for Minecraft Java, Fabric Loader/API, portal rendering and routing, multiple worlds or dimensions, independent weather/time, replay and camera paths, deterministic actor or mob control, world editing/reset, performance/shaders, recording and Blender/compositing fallbacks. Compare at least Minecraft 1.20.1 and 1.21.1, then select a provisional baseline. Do not infer combined compatibility merely because individual projects list the same Minecraft version. Record exact version candidates, dependencies, licences, URLs, publisher, dates, access date, contradictions and evidence tier. Label every untested stack as untested.

Cover every one of the thirteen scenes in the scene implementation matrix. For each scene specify the preferred live method, exact tool/mod candidate, deterministic controls, camera method, VFX or composite method, fallback, linked proof-of-concept test and evidence status. Research existing tools before proposing custom development.

Design a resettable take-controller for complex performances. Prefer a project datapack and vanilla commands first, then Carpet/Scarpet and actor tools. Define `setup`, `arm`, `action`, `reset` and `cleanup` procedures; entity tags and marks; fixed time/weather; spawn suppression; scoreboards; NBT/state control; world/region restoration; synchronisation cues; and safe handling of the wild wolf, tamed unnamed wolf, named Moss, skeleton, creeper, fire and dropped items. Do not rely on natural RNG. Recommend a custom mod only where a documented test proves the existing stack cannot satisfy a scripted requirement.

Create a complete text-first storyboard designed for my aphantasia. Do not generate any image. Provide at least two panels per scene and more for complex actions, match cuts and portal transitions. Every panel must literally state the exact frame, 3x3 composition, camera position/height/FOV or lens, foreground/midground/background, subject marks, facing and eyeline, movement direction, lighting direction, environment, continuity anchors, transition, audio cue and capture purpose. Add compact ASCII overhead or elevation diagrams wherever spatial relationships are difficult to infer. Preserve `MATCH PATH`, `MATCH FRAME A`, granite-seam geography, portal topology, Moss state and the Scene 11–12 creeper route.

Research and recommend the best current image-generation/editing model for a later hand-drawn storyboard-sketch pass. Compare instruction adherence, composition preservation, multi-reference support and character/location consistency. Design a five-panel consistency test based on actual Minecraft or Blender greybox screenshots transformed into one restrained graphite-and-ink storyboard aesthetic. Do not generate, embed or claim any image now.

Update the Production plan only where synthesis is needed; keep detailed evidence in the dedicated research and matrix files. Complete the storyboard plan, technical research, source ledger, claims map, toolchain matrix, scene implementation matrix, test matrix, storyboard panel ledger, shot list, camera paths, location plan/cards, asset register, build budget, risk register and build log. Keep capture and audio-capture logs untouched. Never claim installation, test success, completed builds, footage, rights clearance or selected takes without real evidence.
