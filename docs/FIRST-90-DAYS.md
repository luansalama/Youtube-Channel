# First 90 days — capability ladder

The goal is not maximum upload frequency. It is to establish a repeatable quality floor while building reusable world, capture, and editorial systems.

## Production 1 — contained anomaly

**Target:** 8–12 minutes, one hero location, one corridor/room kit, one unseen force, one decisive reinterpretation.

Build objectives:

- establish project naming, shot IDs, capture logs, and export QC;
- create one reusable material/prop kit;
- test narration recording and caption pipeline;
- learn one reliable cinematic camera workflow;
- finish rather than expand.

Avoid villages, many characters, giant exterior terrain, bespoke mobs, complicated multiplayer blocking, or lore that requires sequels to work.

## Production 2 — spatial expansion

**Target:** 12–18 minutes, reuse at least half of Production 1's technical setup, add one exterior transition and one Blender-enhanced shot.

Build objectives:

- improve environmental continuity;
- introduce a repeated symbol or location family;
- create a sound motif library;
- test one title/thumbnail hypothesis with clear pre-publish rationale;
- begin a reusable behind-the-scenes capture habit.

## Production 3 — controlled investigation

**Target:** 15–22 minutes, two linked locations, multiple evidence types, one character knowledge reversal.

Build objectives:

- use a formal continuity bible;
- test a more complex clue chain without adding world scale;
- establish a reliable private-upload/QC/release process;
- produce a sponsor-safe one-sheet using verified results only;
- decide which series format deserves a second cycle.

## Weekly rhythm while solo

Use phases rather than forcing every department into every week:

1. develop and research;
2. outline and script;
3. pre-produce and build;
4. capture;
5. edit and sound;
6. master, package, release;
7. postmortem and recovery.

Batch reusable work—ambient recordings, modular props, title-card systems, export presets—but never batch story judgement merely to appear productive.

## Success criteria after 90 days

- at least one fully finished video and a clearly selected next idea or paused follow-up;
- actual production-hour data;
- a reusable Minecraft/Blender location kit;
- a proven master/caption/upload checklist;
- an evidence-based understanding of which hooks and moments viewers respond to;
- no dependence on generated final imagery or unreviewed autonomous publishing.
